<div style="text-align: left;">
<p>Final group project for the course Introduction to Multimedia (IF687) course from Centre of Informatics (Cin) of UFPE.</p>

<p>I assumed the <b>Core Game Designer</b> and <b>Lead Developer roles</b>.</p>

<p>In this game, the idea is that you are a Baker Knight, so the weapons
you use are also food! For this MVP we implemented the mechanic of "consuming"
your weapon to heal you.
</p>

<p>
In a more developed version, the idea would be to be able to craft varied
food weapons and equipments, which would be consumable for many different buffs.
</p>
